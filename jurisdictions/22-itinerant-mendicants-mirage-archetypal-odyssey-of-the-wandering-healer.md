# The Itinerant Mendicant's Mirage: The Archetypal Odyssey of the Wandering Healer

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** The romanticized figure of the wandering outsider who trades wisdom, healing, or insight for subsistence — a mirage in the sense that this archetype is often invoked to dignify economic precarity (no stable income, no institutional support) rather than to actually provide for the person living it.

**Key mechanism:** Cultural mythology (the wise wanderer, the healer who owns nothing) is used to reframe structural exclusion from stable livelihood as spiritual choice or calling, obscuring the lack of material support behind the archetype.
