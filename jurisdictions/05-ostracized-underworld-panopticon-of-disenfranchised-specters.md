# The Ostracized Underworld Panopticon of Disenfranchised Specters

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** The condition of people rendered socially invisible or "ghosted" by systems (undocumented status, criminal record, homelessness, estrangement) who are nonetheless still tracked, flagged, and surveilled by those same systems — visible to power, invisible to community.

**Key mechanism:** Formal disenfranchisement (no vote, no standing, no recognized voice) is paired with informal, often invisible tracking (databases, background checks, watch-lists), producing a class that is watched but not heard.
