# Biopolitical Panic Room of Pathologized Intimacy

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A retreat into diagnosis and clinical language as the only sanctioned way to name relational or emotional distress, such that intimacy itself becomes something to be managed, medicated, or treated rather than simply lived — with the "panic room" offering safety only by converting the relationship into a condition.

**Key mechanism:** Ordinary relational pain is reclassified as pathology; the vocabulary of therapy/diagnosis becomes the only legitimate channel for expressing need, narrowing what intimacy is allowed to look like.
