# The Panoptic Domestic Purgatory: The Schmoozer's Domestic Pan-Carceral Cell

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A household or relational space that functions as a holding cell dressed up in sociability — where charm, small talk, and performed friendliness ("schmoozing") are the price of continued residence or belonging, and where surveillance of one's behavior never actually lifts despite the congenial surface.

**Key mechanism:** Social performance (being liked, being easy, being agreeable) becomes a survival requirement inside a space that is otherwise confining; the charm is not freely given warmth but a toll paid to avoid worse treatment.
