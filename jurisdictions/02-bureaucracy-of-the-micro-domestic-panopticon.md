# Bureaucracy of the Micro-Domestic Panopticon

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A regime of surveillance and paperwork that extends state or institutional scrutiny into the smallest details of a household — chores, routines, spending, caregiving — so that domestic life itself becomes a site of monitoring and self-justification.

**Key mechanism:** Small-scale, everyday domestic behavior is rendered visible/auditable (via forms, case notes, means-testing, wellness checks, etc.), inducing constant self-policing in anticipation of inspection.
