# The Dissipative Slump-State Niche: The Pulsed-Burst Service Sink

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A structural role or labor niche defined by irregular, on-demand bursts of intense output followed by unpaid, unstructured idle time — energy demanded in spikes, with the "slump" periods absorbed entirely by the worker rather than compensated by the system.

**Key mechanism:** Common to gig/on-call/service labor: value is extracted in short high-intensity bursts (a shift, a rush, a callout), while the recovery time between bursts is externalized onto the worker as unpaid downtime.
