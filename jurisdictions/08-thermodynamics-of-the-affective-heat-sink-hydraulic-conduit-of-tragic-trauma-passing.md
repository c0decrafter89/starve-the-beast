# The Thermodynamics of the Affective Heat Sink: The Hydraulic Conduit of Tragic Trauma-Passing

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A metaphorical system in which one person (or role) absorbs and dissipates the excess emotional "heat" (distress, trauma, dysfunction) generated elsewhere, so the system as a whole can keep functioning — at the cost of that person's own equilibrium.

**Key mechanism:** Distress flows downhill, from those with more power/stability to those with less, along the path of least resistance (the family scapegoat, the junior employee, the eldest daughter); the sink absorbs but rarely gets to release in turn.
