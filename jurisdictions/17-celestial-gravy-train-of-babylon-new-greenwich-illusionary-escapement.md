# The Celestial Gravy Train of Babylon: The New Greenwich Illusionary Escapement

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A mythologized image of limitless, effortless abundance available "elsewhere" or "elsewhen" (a new capital, a new prime meridian, a new center of time and value) that functions as a release valve for discontent — an escapement mechanism, in the clockwork sense, that lets just enough pressure out to keep the larger mechanism ticking.

**Key mechanism:** The promise of an accessible utopia (often geographically or temporally displaced) discharges accumulated frustration with the current system without requiring any actual redistribution of the wealth or time it withholds.
