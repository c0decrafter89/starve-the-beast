# The Jurisprudence of Affective Usury: The Doctrine of Implied Quasi-Fiduciary Entrapment

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A conceptual legal framework describing how one party can extract ongoing emotional "interest payments" from another by implying a duty of care or loyalty that was never formally agreed to — trust is manufactured, then billed against indefinitely.

**Key mechanism:** An implied (never contracted) obligation of trust/loyalty is asserted after the fact; the "debt" is emotional rather than financial, but functions the same way usury does — extraction that compounds and is never considered fully repaid.
