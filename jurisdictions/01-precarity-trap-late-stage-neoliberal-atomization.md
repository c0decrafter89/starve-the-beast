# Precarity Trap under Late-Stage Neoliberal Atomization

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** The condition in which individuals are stripped of collective structures (unions, extended family, stable community, guaranteed welfare) and made solely responsible for absorbing economic shocks, while the market that produces those shocks bears none of the cost. Atomization isn't incidental — it's the mechanism that makes precarity personal instead of political.

**Key mechanism:** Risk is privatized and individualized; solidarity infrastructure is dismantled or discouraged; instability is reframed as a personal failure of planning, resilience, or character.
