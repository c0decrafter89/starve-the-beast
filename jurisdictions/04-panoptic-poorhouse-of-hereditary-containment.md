# Panoptic Poorhouse of Hereditary Containment

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A structure — economic, legal, or familial — that keeps poverty circulating within the same lineage across generations while making every member feel individually watched and individually blamed for not escaping it.

**Key mechanism:** Barriers to exit (debt, inheritance of obligation, lack of asset transfer) combine with visibility/judgment (means-testing, stigma, surveillance of "the poor") to make containment feel like personal failure rather than structural design.
