# The Panoptic Purgatory of the Standby Car: The Imperial Proving-Ground Trap

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A holding state of enforced readiness — always "on call," always available, never fully released — in which a person must continuously prove their worth or loyalty to a larger system without ever being granted stable membership or reward within it.

**Key mechanism:** Conditional, provisional status (probation, contractor status, immigration status, "at-will" standing) is maintained indefinitely; the proving never concludes because concluding it would require the system to extend real security.
