# The Neo-Feudal Oligarchy of the Absentee Dynastic Heir: The Ghost-Ship Plutocracy

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A concentration of inherited wealth and power held by heirs who are functionally absent from the systems they control — a plutocracy steered, in effect, by no one actively present, like a ship still afloat and still setting the course while unmanned.

**Key mechanism:** Wealth and authority pass by inheritance rather than active stewardship or accountability; the people bearing the consequences of decisions rarely encounter the people (nominally) making them.
