# The Jurisprudence of Affective Usury (Expanded): Unjust Enrichment via Emotional Speculation + Tort of Coerced Reciprocity

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** An expansion of entry #06 (Jurisprudence of Affective Usury): frames the same implied-trust extraction explicitly as (a) one party being unjustly enriched by speculating on another's emotional investment without ever formally acknowledging the debt, and (b) a "tort" in which reciprocity is coerced — given under pressure rather than offered freely, and then treated as proof of consent.

**Key mechanism:** Two-part harm — enrichment without acknowledged obligation, plus manufactured consent to keep giving — that together make the extraction look voluntary from the outside while functioning as compulsion from the inside.
