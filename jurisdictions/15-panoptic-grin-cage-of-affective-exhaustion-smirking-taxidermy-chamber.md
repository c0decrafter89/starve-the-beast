# The Panoptic Grin-Cage of Affective Exhaustion: The Smirking Taxidermy Chamber

[← Back to index](../jurisdictions.md)

**Core definition:** A social or professional requirement to perform positive affect (smiling, enthusiasm, agreeableness) continuously and convincingly, regardless of actual internal state, until the performed emotion hollows out the person performing it — a mask that stops coming off.

**Key mechanism:** Emotional labor is compulsory and one-directional (customer service, caregiving, "culture fit"); genuine affect is replaced by a fixed, surveilled performance of affect, and exhaustion accumulates precisely because the mask is never permitted to slip.
